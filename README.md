# Christian Villamizar | Engineering Portfolio

- **Drawings and models:** Ten drawings and models from Engineering Design & Presentation coursework, 2021 to 2022, including a box assembly, micro vehicle, mini sawhorse, martini glass, paperweight, and several orthographic drafting exercises.
- **Skills:** CAD tools, software, certifications, and coursework.
- **Leadership and experience:** ASME and IEEE at Texas A&M, Freshman Leaders in Progress, engineering camps, and work experience.
